| 2026-11-10 | **D-12. All data is kept permanently.** No retention or deletion job drops any class of record: not the three logs or their partitions, webhook payloads, attachments, or lead and customer records. Old S3 versions are kept too. **Scope, confirmed by Sarthak the same day:** this covers business and personal records. The platform's working data keeps its designed expiry: saved-once keys (48 h, or 30 days for sync), job-queue rows (a reset job is deleted on completion, ADR-001 §7a), rate-limit counters and ended sessions (sign-ins themselves are kept, in `event_log`). **Risk noted:** the DPDP Act expects personal data to be erased once its purpose ends or consent is withdrawn, so keeping customer and lead data forever is a regulatory risk. That stays open for a data-protection lawyer (D-09). The audit log keeps references, not copies (M6-16), so erasure on request stays possible later without rewriting append-only rows. This replaces "retention jobs per D-12" in plan.md's M6-16 | Sarthak, via the coordinator | [ADR-005](architecture/adr/005-database.md) §8, ADR-004 (S3 versions, Object Lock) (RAD-30) |
