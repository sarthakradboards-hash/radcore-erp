# ADR-010: GST returns and their settlement

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-27
- Ticket: RAD-151 (M5-01), from the M5 plan Sarthak approved on 27 Nov ("Approve as drafted"). The design basis for
  M5-03 (reverse charge), M5-04 (GSTR-1 sections), M5-05 (the GSTR-1 portal file), M5-06 (GSTR-3B), M5-07 (GST settled
  in the books) and M5-15 (e-invoice fields, D-03).
- Builds on: [ADR-009](009-accounts.md) §2 (input credit and its reasons), §5 (the filed-month lock, the snapshot),
  §14 (opening invoices); [ADR-006](006-ledger.md) §2 (accounts, posting rules), §3 (kinds), §6, §7 (books check);
  [ADR-007](007-sales-documents.md) §5 (advances), §6 (credit notes, the section 34 limit), §7 (the invoice);
  [ADR-001](001-layers.md) §3 (lib-gst reads returns from views).
- Source: the register (RC-057, RC-059, RC-107, RC-108, RC-109, RC-116); plan §6's M5 lines; CA Group A, sections A1
  to A6 (CA-01 to CA-52, docs/source/10-Questions-for-CA-and-Counsel.md); D-03, D-08, D-10.

## Context

v1's GSTR-1 left out nil-rated and exempt supplies, advances, e-commerce supplies and amendments, and filed 0% sales in
the wrong table (RC-107). Its file was never checked against the portal's own rules (RC-059). Its 3B had two lines, no
set-off across heads, and ignored the credit brought forward (RC-108). GST was never settled in the books, so nothing
tied a month's return to the ledger (RC-109). There was no reverse charge (RC-057), and an e-invoice's IRN couldn't
be entered or printed (RC-116).

M2 to M4 already freeze what a return needs on each document: an invoice keeps the buyer's GSTIN, state and place of
supply, and each line its HSN, rate, tax by head and cess; a credit note names its invoice line; a bill keeps the
supplier's GSTIN and invoice date, and ADR-009 §2 records each bill and expense line's credit claimed, and why not.
This ADR says how M5 turns those into returns, settles them in the books, and ties each filed month to the ledger.
Where a rule waits on the CA, the default is the stricter reading, held in a setting or a dated table (D-08); §11
lists each with its CA question.

## Decision

### 1. Where it lives (ADR-001 §3)

- **lib-gst** builds the returns. It reads documents only through views in packages/db (ADR-002 §3's SQL zone):
  `gst_outward_lines` (invoice and credit-note lines with their document's frozen buyer, place of supply and dates)
  and `gst_inward_lines` (bill, supplier-return and expense lines with ADR-009 §2's credit columns). It holds the
  reverse-charge rules (§4) and the set-off (§7) as pure functions.
- **lib-accounts** marks returns filed (ADR-009 §5), takes the snapshot, and posts the settlement (§8), calling lib-gst
  for the figures. lib-gst never calls lib-accounts.
- **A return is built, never stored, until it's filed.** Building reads the month's documents; marking filed stores
  what was built in the snapshot (§9).

### 2. What each supply is (CA-07)

- **The supply kind of a rate.** `gst_rates` gains `supply_kind`: `taxable`, `nil` (a 0% rate), `exempt` or `non_gst`,
  each row naming its source (M2-12's convention). A line's kind is its rate row's, frozen on the line at posting.
  Zero-rated supplies (exports, SEZ) are D-10's, in M9.
- **0% is nil, not B2B at zero (RC-107).** A `nil`, `exempt` or `non_gst` line goes to GSTR-1 Table 8 and 3B 3.1(c) or
  (e), never to Table 4, 5 or 7. An invoice may mix kinds; each line goes where its kind says.
- **E-commerce (CA-07, CA-23).** An invoice may name the e-commerce operator it was sold through (`ecommerce_gstin`,
  M5-04 adds it, null by default). Shopify is our own store, not an operator. When set, the invoice's lines are
  reported with that GSTIN (Table 4C or Table 7's operator rows, and Table 14). Until CA-07 says such sales exist, the
  field is empty and those tables are empty.
- **Advances (CA-09).** An advance for goods carries no GST (ADR-007 §5), so Table 11 is empty for goods. An advance for
  services, when the service module brings one, reports in Table 11A and its adjustment in 11B; until then the tables
  are built empty.

### 3. GSTR-1 (M5-04, RC-107)

Every table is built from `gst_outward_lines` for the month of the document's business date. B2B means the buyer has a
GSTIN, a composition dealer included.

| Table | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| 4A, 4C | B2B invoices, invoice by invoice: the buyer's GSTIN, number, date, value, place of supply, and per rate the taxable value and tax by head and cess. 4C: those with an `ecommerce_gstin` |
| 5 | B2C large: inter-state invoices to buyers with no GSTIN whose invoice value is over `gst.b2cl_limit` (1,00,000 by default, the limit from 1 August 2024; CA confirms), invoice by invoice |
| 7 | B2C others: every other sale to a buyer with no GSTIN, summed by place of supply, rate (and operator GSTIN) |
| 8 | Nil-rated, exempt and non-GST supplies, by inter- or intra-state and registered or not, with no tax |
| 9A | Amendments to invoices of earlier months already filed (§5) |
| 9B | Credit notes: to registered buyers (CDNR) note by note; to buyers with no GSTIN only where the invoice was B2C large (CDNUR); a credit note on any other B2C invoice lowers Table 7 in its own month |
| 11A, 11B | Advances for services and their adjustment (§2); empty for goods |
| 12 | HSN summary, in two parts, B2B and B2C: per HSN at `gst.hsn_digits` (6 by default, CA-17), its unit as a UQC, quantity, taxable value and tax by head and cess, net of credit notes |
| 13 | Documents issued, per series: the first and last number of the month, the count, and the count cancelled. Invoices, credit notes and job-work delivery challans (ADR-008 §11), from `number_counters` and each document's status. Receipt and refund vouchers only once Table 11 has a service advance: our receipt series (`RV`) are payment receipts, not section 31's vouchers |
| 14 | Supplies made through an e-commerce operator, by operator |

- **Not reported:** a credit note past section 34's time limit (ADR-007 §6), whose tax the company bears; opening
  invoices from Vyapar (ADR-009 §14), already reported there. A credit note against an opening invoice is reported in
  its own month, naming Vyapar's number and date.
- **Units.** Each item unit maps to a UQC code (`units.uqc`, M5-04 adds it and seeds the common ones); an item whose
  unit has none can't be reported (`uqc_missing`), so the dry run of M5-16's items step lists it.
- **Cess** comes from the lines' cess columns. Until CA-50 says a good attracts cess, every cess is zero.

### 4. Reverse charge (M5-03, RC-057, CA-04, CA-06)

- **The rules, by date.** `rcm_rules` in lib-gst: a SAC prefix (or an HSN prefix, for section 9(4)'s notified goods),
  the supplier condition it applies to (`unregistered`, `any`, `not_body_corporate`), `effective_from`, source and note.
  Never deleted, corrected in place with a reason, edited under `gst:write`. The migration seeds the rows CA-06 names
  (goods transport agency, legal services by an advocate, sponsorship, security services), each marked unconfirmed
  until the CA signs it (D-08). The rate is GST's own, from `gst_rates` by SAC.
- **Where a SAC comes from.** An expense line and a service bill's `charge` line gain a SAC (M5-03 adds it); a category
  may name a default. A line whose SAC and supplier match a rule in force on the supplier's invoice date is reverse
  charge: the supplier's bill carries no tax (`rcm_bill_has_tax` if it does), and lib-gst works the tax out at the rate
  in force, as CGST and SGST if the supplier is in our state, else IGST (CA-06).
- **What posts (F31).** At booking, the tax is owed and the credit waits for the payment: Dr the expense, Dr RCM Credit
  Pending (by head), Cr the supplier, Cr RCM Payable (by head). At settlement (§8) the RCM tax is paid in cash only, and
  the pending credit moves to Input GST, available for set-off from the next return. Whether the credit is claimable at
  all is ADR-009 §2's test, with the supplier's kind passed over (a reverse-charge supplier is usually unregistered): a
  blocked category still owes the tax but claims nothing.
- **New system accounts** (ADR-006 §2, control `gst`): `rcm_cgst`, `rcm_sgst`, `rcm_igst`, `rcm_cess` (RCM Payable,
  liability) and `rcm_credit_pending` (asset). Posting rules: `gst` gains `gst_payment` and `gst_settlement` (§8).
- **Services from abroad (CA-06; Sarthak, 27 Nov).** Ad spend billed from abroad (Meta's Irish company, for example) is
  an import of services, under reverse charge, even when paid in rupees by card. A party may be an `overseas` supplier
  (`parties.overseas`, M5-03 adds it): a supplier only, with no GSTIN or PAN, and state code `96` (other country), which
  the parties CHECKs allow only for it. It takes services in rupees only: an expense, or a service bill of `charge`
  lines, never a purchase order, a goods receipt or a `goods` line (`overseas_services_only`), and no currency other
  than the rupee. Every such line is reverse charge, as IGST, whatever `rcm_rules` says; its credit is ADR-009 §2's test
  with the supplier's kind passed over. Foreign suppliers of goods, and foreign currency, stay in M9 (D-10).

### 5. Amendments and corrections (CA-10, CA-11)

- **Value goes down:** a credit note (ADR-007 §6), in its own month (Table 9B).
- **Value goes up:** a new invoice for the difference, naming the first in its notes, in its own month. Debit notes
  to customers aren't built; CA-10 says whether they're needed.
- **A wrong buyer GSTIN, after GSTR-1 is filed:** an `invoice_amendments` row (the invoice, the old and new GSTIN, the
  reason, who, when), under `gst:file`, reported in Table 9A of the month it's made. The new GSTIN must have the same
  state, so the tax heads don't change (`amendment_changes_heads`); a change of state or place of supply is a credit
  note and a new invoice. The posted invoice keeps its frozen fields; its print shows "Amended" with the new GSTIN.
  An invoice not yet in a filed GSTR-1 can't be amended (`not_filed`): it's cancelled and raised again (ADR-009 §5).
- **The time limit (CA-11):** an amendment is refused after 30 November following the end of the invoice's financial
  year (`amendment_window_closed`), the limit `gst.amendment_last_day` holds as a month and day.

### 6. GSTR-3B (M5-06, RC-108, CA-13)

Built from the same views for the month, with the credit brought forward from the ledger.

| 3B table | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| 3.1(a) | Taxable outward supplies (`taxable` lines), net of the month's credit notes |
| 3.1(b) | Zero-rated supplies: empty until D-10 |
| 3.1(c) | Nil-rated and exempt outward supplies |
| 3.1(d) | Inward supplies under reverse charge (§4): value and tax |
| 3.1(e) | Non-GST outward supplies |
| 3.1.1 | Supplies through an operator paying tax under section 9(5): empty until CA-07 |
| 3.2 | Of 3.1(a), inter-state supplies to buyers with no GSTIN and to composition dealers, by place of supply |
| 4(A)(2) | Credit on services imported from an `overseas` supplier (§4), in the month the tax is paid |
| 4(A)(3) | Credit on other reverse-charge supplies, in the month the tax is paid (§4) |
| 4(A)(5) | All other credit: every line of a regular supplier with the right heads, its claimed credit plus any blocked credit; net of supplier returns (debit notes) and inward cancels in their own month (ADR-009 §5, CA-52) |
| 4(B)(1) | Credit reversed for good: section 17(5)'s blocked credit (ADR-009 §2's `blocked`) |
| 4(B)(2) | Other reversals: entered by the person with a reason (Rule 37's 180 days, for example); 0 by default |
| 4(C) | Net credit: 4(A) less 4(B) |
| 4(D)(1) | Credit reclaimed that was reversed under 4(B)(2) earlier: entered with a reason |
| 4(D)(2) | Credit not available: place-of-supply (`state`) and section 16(4) (`time_barred`, §10) |
| 5 | Inward supplies from composition dealers, and exempt, nil-rated and non-GST inward supplies, inter and intra |
| 5.1 | Interest and late fee, entered from the portal's figure |
| 6.1 | Tax payable and paid: the set-off (§7) |

- **Where credit with no claim lands (CA-13):** `blocked` → 4(A)(5) and 4(B)(1); `state` and `time_barred` → 4(D)(2);
  a composition supplier's bill → 5; an unregistered supplier's bill not under reverse charge → nowhere. The CA signs
  this mapping box by box before the first real return.
- **4(B)(2) and 4(D)(1)** are `gst_adjustments` rows on the month's draft 3B (amount by head, reason, who), and post
  with the settlement (§8): a reversal Dr ITC Reversed (Reclaimable), Cr Input GST; a reclaim the reverse. ITC
  Reversed (Reclaimable) is a new system account, `itc_reversed` (asset, control `gst`).

### 7. The set-off (M5-06, CA-14)

lib-gst's `setOff` works out 6.1 from the month's liability by head and the credit by head (Input GST balances at the
month's end, after 4(B) and 4(D)), in this order, which uses all the IGST credit before any other (section 49A) and
leaves as little cash as the rules allow:
1. IGST credit against IGST;
2. what's left of it against CGST, then SGST, each first up to what its own credit can't cover, then any more;
3. CGST credit against CGST, SGST credit against SGST;
4. what's left of CGST credit against IGST still owed, then SGST credit against it;
5. cess credit against cess only;
6. reverse-charge tax: cash only, never credit;
7. the rest in cash, by head. Credit left over carries forward. CGST credit never pays SGST, nor the reverse.

The set-off is computed, not typed; a person can't change it. If the cash ledger (§8) holds less than the cash part,
the month can't be marked filed (`cash_ledger_short`).

### 8. Settled in the books (M5-07, RC-109)

- **Cash into the portal:** a `gst_payments` document (the challan's CPIN and CIN, the date, the bank account, amount by
  head) under `payments:write`. It posts kind `gst_payment`: Dr GST Cash Ledger (by head), Cr Bank. A new system
  account `gst_cash_ledger` (asset, control `gst`), by head through its lines.
- **The settlement:** marking 3B filed (ADR-009 §5) posts, in the same save, a `gst_settlement` entry from the filed
  snapshot's 6.1, dated the filing date (in the open month after, so the month's own lock doesn't stop it): Dr Output
  GST (by head, the month's liability), Dr RCM Payable; Cr Input GST (the credit used), Cr GST Cash Ledger (the cash
  used); and the RCM pending credit moved to Input GST (§4); and the 4(B)(2)/4(D)(1) rows (§6). Interest and late fee:
  Dr GST Interest or GST Late Fee (new ordinary expense accounts), Cr GST Cash Ledger. F30.
- **Its source is the filing row,** so undoing a filing (ADR-009 §5) reverses the settlement in the same save, and
  marking it filed again posts a new one.
- **The kinds** `gst_payment` and `gst_settlement` join ADR-006 §3's list, ADR-009 §5's 3B door list (so a later
  filing locks them), and the posting rules for `gst`.
- **Each return ties to the ledger.** The books check (ADR-006 §7) gains, for each filed month: the snapshot's 3.1 tax
  by head equals the Output GST lines of that month's outward kinds; its 4(A) less 4(B) equals the Input GST lines of
  that month's inward kinds and adjustments; and the GST Cash Ledger equals payments less cash used. Since a filed
  month is locked, a later difference can only come from a bug, and the check names the month.

### 9. The snapshot's return part (ADR-009 §5)

- **GSTR-1:** the portal file as built (§10's JSON) and its SHA-256.
- **GSTR-3B:** every table of §6, the set-off of §7, and the `gst_adjustments` rows, as JSON, with its SHA-256.
- Kept beside ADR-009 §5's ledger summary. A filing undone keeps its snapshot; the next filing has its own.

### 10. The portal file and credit checks (M5-05, RC-059; CA-12)

- **Checked against GSTN's own schema (RC-059).** M5-05 vendors GSTN's published GSTR-1 JSON schema, at its version
  and with its source, and builds the file to it. A test validates every built file against that schema with a JSON
  Schema validator (a dev dependency, with its ADR-003 row and the 14-day rule), and against GSTN's sample files.
- **Before a file is offered,** every GSTIN in it passes the check-digit test (`gstin_check_digit`; the parties CHECK
  only holds the pattern), every amount has two places, every place of supply is a state code, and Table 13's ranges
  agree with the documents in the file.
- **Section 16(4) (CA-12):** a bill or expense whose supplier invoice is of a financial year whose 30 November has
  passed claims no credit (`time_barred`, ADR-009 §2's reasons), and it shows in 4(D)(2).
- **Not built in M5 (CA-12):** matching to GSTR-2B, Rule 37's 180-day reversal and Rules 42 and 43. M5-06's 3B screen
  lists bills unpaid for 180 days with credit claimed, for the CA's monthly check; a reversal is a 4(B)(2) row (§6). If
  the CA wants 2B matched in software, it's an upload of the 2B file through the import engine (ADR-009 §14) and a
  report, a ticket of its own.

### 11. Quarterly filing (CA-03)

Monthly is built (`gst.filing_frequency`). If CA-03 says QRMP, a ticket of its own changes:
- `gst_filings` gains the period (a quarter for 3B and GSTR-1, a month for the invoice furnishing facility);
- the filed-month lock dates move by quarter end for 3B, and for the facility's months by the B2B invoices only, which
  needs the entry trigger to know a B2B invoice (a flag on the entry);
- monthly cash payments (PMT-06) are `gst_payments` with no settlement until the quarter's 3B.
Nothing here is built until then.

### 12. E-invoice fields (M5-15, RC-116, D-03, CA-01)

- **Fields only (D-03).** A posted invoice may have its IRN (64 hexadecimal characters), acknowledgement number and
  date, and the signed QR text entered by hand, once, under `invoices:write` (ADR-007 §2's write-once columns),
  audited. The invoice prints them, the QR as an image from the text.
- **A live IRN blocks cancelling (`irn_live`).** The person cancels the IRN on the portal first (within its 24 hours)
  and records that (`irn_cancelled_at`); only then does the invoice cancel. After the portal's window, the correction
  is a credit note.
- The portal link and e-way bills stay in M9 (M9-03), unless CA-01 or CA-02 says the turnover needs them sooner.

### 13. Lock order (ADR-006 §1, ADR-009 §7)

- **Marking 3B filed with its settlement:** the profile and the period lock `FOR SHARE`, the filed-month row
  `FOR UPDATE`; the filing row; the journal series.
- **A GST payment, and its cancel:** step 1 `FOR SHARE`; the payment; the journal series. A cancel is refused if it
  would take the GST Cash Ledger below zero for any head (`cash_ledger_short`). Since a settlement holds the
  filed-month row `FOR UPDATE` and a cancel holds it `FOR SHARE`, the two take turns, and the second sees the first.
- **An amendment:** step 1 `FOR SHARE`; the invoice `FOR UPDATE`; no series (it posts nothing).
- **A reverse-charge bill or expense:** as ADR-009 §7's posting; the rule is read, not locked.

### New hand-worked figures (ADR-006 §8's conventions; rates are examples)

**F30. A month's set-off** (M5-06, M5-07). Liability: IGST 10,000.00, CGST 6,000.00, SGST 6,000.00. Credit at the
month's end: IGST 12,000.00, CGST 2,000.00, SGST 1,000.00.
- Set-off: IGST credit 10,000.00 against IGST; its remaining 2,000.00 against CGST, whose own credit falls 4,000.00
  short; CGST credit 2,000.00 against CGST; SGST credit 1,000.00 against SGST. Cash: CGST 2,000.00, SGST 5,000.00.
- Entry `gst_settlement`: Dr Output IGST 10,000.00; Dr Output CGST 6,000.00; Dr Output SGST 6,000.00; Cr Input IGST
  12,000.00; Cr Input CGST 2,000.00; Cr Input SGST 1,000.00; Cr GST Cash Ledger 7,000.00. Both sides 22,000.00.
- Variant: CGST's own credit 5,000.00 instead. IGST's remaining 2,000.00 covers CGST's 1,000.00 shortfall, then
  1,000.00 of SGST's 5,000.00 shortfall; cash is SGST 4,000.00 only, and no CGST credit is stranded.

**F31. Freight under reverse charge** (M5-03, M5-07). A goods transport agency in Maharashtra, unregistered, bills
10,000.00; GST 5% under reverse charge.
- Booking, entry `purchase_bill`: Dr Freight 10,000.00; Dr RCM Credit Pending 500.00; Cr Creditors 10,000.00; Cr RCM
  CGST Payable 250.00; Cr RCM SGST Payable 250.00.
- At the month's settlement: Dr RCM CGST Payable 250.00; Dr RCM SGST Payable 250.00; Cr GST Cash Ledger 500.00; and
  Dr Input CGST 250.00; Dr Input SGST 250.00; Cr RCM Credit Pending 500.00. 3B: 3.1(d) 10,000.00 and 500.00; 4(A)(3)
  500.00.

**Tests**, beyond each table's happy path:
- **M5-03:** F31 exactly; a rule dated 1 July applies to a bill dated 1 July and not 30 June; a supplier's bill with tax
  on a reverse-charge line is refused; a blocked category still owes the tax and claims nothing; an overseas supplier's
  expense is IGST reverse charge and lands in 3B 4(A)(2); an overseas supplier on a purchase order or a `goods` line is
  refused `overseas_services_only`.
- **M5-04:** a 0% line in Table 8 and not 4; a B2C inter-state invoice at the limit in Table 7 and 0.01 over in Table
  5; a credit note on a B2C large invoice in CDNUR and on a small one in Table 7; a late credit note and an opening
  invoice in no table, and a credit note on an opening invoice in 9B with Vyapar's number; Table 13's counts with a
  cancelled invoice; an amendment that changes the state refused; HSN at 4 and 6 digits.
- **M5-05:** every built file passes GSTN's schema and its samples; a bad check digit is refused before the file.
- **M5-06:** F30 and its variant; CGST credit never pays SGST; RCM tax is all cash; `cash_ledger_short`; each of
  ADR-009 §2's reasons lands in its box; a supplier return in its month lowers 4(A)(5); a bill past 16(4) claims
  nothing and shows in 4(D)(2).
- **M5-07:** F30's and F31's entries; undoing the filing reverses the settlement; the books check fails when a filed
  month's ledger differs from its snapshot; payments less cash used equals the cash ledger.
- **M5-15:** an IRN entered once and printed; a second entry refused; cancelling with a live IRN refused, and allowed
  once its cancellation is recorded.

## Consequences

- Each Wave C ticket has its tables, boxes and entries fixed before it starts. The CA's answers change rows, settings
  and the box mapping, not tables (D-08).
- A filed 3B posts its settlement at once, so the books show what was paid and what credit carries forward the day it
  was filed, and the books check ties each filed month to its return.
- Services from abroad paid in rupees are booked with their reverse charge from M5 (Sarthak, 27 Nov); goods from
  abroad and foreign currency still wait for M9.

## Alternatives considered

- **Build the returns from the ledger.** The ledger has no buyer GSTIN, place of supply or HSN. The documents do, and
  the books check proves the two agree.
- **Store each return's figures as documents are posted.** Every correction would have to update them, and a bug would
  go unseen. Building from the documents and checking against the ledger catches it.
- **Let the person type the set-off.** The portal lets them; a wrong split strands credit or pays cash it needn't. The
  rules make one best split, so it's computed.
- **Amend any field of a filed invoice.** A change of state changes the tax heads, which an amendment can't undo; a
  credit note and a new invoice can.
